Spider Man

Powers and Abilities

• Superhuman Strength and Agility: He can lift heavy objects, leap great distances, and move with acrobatic reflexes.
• Wall-Crawling: He can cling to walls and ceilings with his bare hands and feet.
• Spider-Sense: A precognitive sixth sense that warns him of nearby danger.
• Web-Shooting: He uses high-tech mechanical web-shooters of his own invention (or organic webbing in select adaptations) to swing across New York .

Origins and Identity

• Secret Identity: Spider-Man is the alias of Peter Benjamin Parker, an orphan raised by his Aunt May and Uncle Ben in Queens, New York City.
• The Bite: Peter gains arachnid-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive (or genetically altered) spider.
• Core Motto: Guided by the tragic loss of Uncle Ben, he lives by the creed: "With great power comes great responsibility"

Live-Action Portrayals

• Tobey Maguire: Starred in Sam Raimi’s trilogy starting with Spider-Man (2002).
• Andrew Garfield: Starred in The Amazing Spider-Man films (2012–2014).
• Tom Holland: Portrays the character within the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) starting from Captain America: Civil War and his standalone trilogy.
